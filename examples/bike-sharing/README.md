# Example: bike-sharing demand

A worked example of the canvas at the **planning stage**, after `/grill-question` and `/explore-data`, before any modelling. Blocks 9–11 show the intended shape, not results.

Data: the [Bike Sharing Dataset](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/275/bike+sharing+dataset) (UCI Machine Learning Repository, CC BY 4.0): daily and hourly rental counts of Capital Bikeshare in Washington, D.C., 2011–2012, with weather and calendar information.

The scenario (an operations team repositioning bikes) is fictional and only illustrates how the blocks are filled.

- [CANVAS.md](CANVAS.md)

## Use it with the lessons

The [seven lessons](../../docs/lessons.md) can be done on this example. Work in a repository of your own, not in this one:

1. Create an empty repository, for example `bike-sharing-practice`, and open it with your agent.
2. Download the [dataset archive](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/static/public/275/bike+sharing+dataset.zip) and unzip `hour.csv`, `day.csv` and `Readme.txt` into `data/`. The canvas uses `hour.csv`.
3. Start with lesson 1. Fill in your own canvas, then compare it with [CANVAS.md](CANVAS.md).

| Lessons | What this example gives you |
|---|---|
| 1–4 | A reference answer: blocks 1–8 as they could look after the question, the data and the plan are settled. Yours may differ; compare the reasons, not the wording. |
| 5–7 | No reference. Block 9 lists the planned cards; findings, recommendation and memo are yours to produce. |

The files the canvas mentions (`DECISIONS.md`, `GLOSSARY.md`, `docs/data-fact-sheet.md`) are created in your repository by `/setup-ds-project` and `/explore-data`; they are not part of this example.
